Brio concept, step 4

Three layout directions, one brand

The palette, the typefaces and the icon language are identical in all three and come from DESIGN-artifacts.md, sampled from the pixels of brand plate A. Only the composition changes: density, rhythm, how the card is built, how much air, where the badge sits, how large the photograph runs. The content is real product content taken from the wireframes, so the choice is made on the thing itself rather than on filler.

#F7F8FC background #FFFFFF surface #1D1D1D ink #A4BEF3 soft accent #346FEC action #5B6171 muted Inter Tight + Inter Solar linear icons

Single column, wide air

Direction 01

What its layout answers. A1, calm and plain: one narrow column, one thing at a time, nothing competing for the eye, so the surface lowers the guard instead of raising it. A3, restraint expressed as emptiness rather than as decoration. A4 gets the most room here, because the read and the next step can be set large and still sit inside one card. The risk to weigh: Priya lives on this screen daily, and a single column pushes participation and the privacy line below the fold on a laptop.

Working desk, two columns

Direction 02 - chosen

Chosen 18 August 2026, and built out as the concept stand on Concept.

What its layout answers. The daily reality of the primary persona: the pulse, the participation, the privacy line and the next program are all on one screen without scrolling, which is the operator setup under five minutes and the owner review under two. A4 still holds, because the read and the next step stay inside the pulse card and never split into a bare metric tile. The risk to weigh: density is the shortest path to the clinical, surveillance-flavoured feel that A1 rules out, so every row added to the rail has to earn its place.

Evidence band, proof always on screen

Direction 03

What its layout answers. A2, proof shown rather than privacy promised: what the owner can and cannot see is a permanent panel on the right, not a link somebody has to go and find. A5 gets its own room, because the photograph runs as a band at real size instead of as a thumbnail, which is where the human register lives. Cards carry a line rather than a fill, so the page reads structural, which is A3. The risk to weigh: the band costs vertical space on a 360px screen, and a permanent proof panel competes with the one main action the wireframe contract allows per screen.

Values used on this page, and where each came from

  • Palette: the six values above, taken from the pixels of design/concept/assets/brand-plate-a.png, not from the captions printed on it. Recorded in the root DESIGN-artifacts.md.
  • Accent discipline: #346FEC marks what can be acted on and nothing else. It is never used as text on the page background, where it measures 4.27 and fails AA; white on it measures 4.53 and passes.
  • Typefaces: Inter Tight for display and Inter for body, chosen against the character of plate A, with tabular figures because the score and participation are numbers that carry meaning.
  • Icons: Solar linear, one weight, inline SVG, no icon font and no CDN.
  • Photograph: Unsplash photo-1630487656049-6db93a53a7e9. The same image runs in all three directions on purpose, so the only thing that changes between them is the composition.
  • Copy: taken from the wireframes at nodes 4.0 Dashboard and 5.0 Program library, which are governed by voice/docs/voice.md. Nothing here is written fresh.
