The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis, and that is a decision rather than an omission. A metric never turns red or green: honest signals over flattering ones, and a colour on a number is a judgement the operator has not asked for.

When to use it

Participation on the dashboard, the size of a team on the roster, the length of a program in the library. Seventeen screens.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Always with its unit, and always with a sentence near it that says what it means. Tabular figures are on in base.css so the number does not shift the layout as it changes.

Not this, use Pulse card instead. Do not use a metric for the team score. The score comes with an interpretation and a next step, and that whole thing is a Pulse card.
