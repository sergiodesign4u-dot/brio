Spark

Five bars. The last two are the ones in the action colour, because they are the cycles the operator is being asked about.

Anatomy

  • .wf-sparkthe row: 56px tall, bars aligned to the bottom
  • .barone cycle, 18px wide, the soft accent fill
  • .bar.hian emphasised cycle, the action fill
  • .bar.h32 to .bar.h60the height of a bar, which is data and not styling

Variants and sizes

Content

One value: five bars, the last two emphasised. The heights are the data, so they are classes rather than inline styles, and there is no second form.

five cycles
every occurrence

The empty cells, and why they are empty

No axis for a different number of cycles. Brio shows five, which is what a trend needs to be a trend and few enough to read without a legend.

When to use it

Beside the score, on the dashboard and inside the proof disclosure. It is the only chart in the product and it has no axes, no labels and no tooltip: it answers one question, is this going up or down.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Five bars, always. The two in the action colour are the most recent cycles.

Not this, use Metric instead. If a number needs explaining rather than trending, use a Metric with its unit and a sentence.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive: there is nothing to hover and nothing to open. A tooltip here would be a drill-down, and drill-down is deferred permanently for the MVP because it breaks the privacy model.
