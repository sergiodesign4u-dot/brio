When to use it

Every operator screen. It repeats the privacy sentence one more time, quietly, at the bottom, which is the third principle applied to a place nobody reads by accident but everybody can check.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Two links at most. This is not the place to put the product map.

Not this, use Public footer instead. The public pages carry a Public footer instead, with four columns and a trust line, because a visitor is looking for things and an operator is not.
