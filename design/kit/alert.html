The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis: there is one severity in this product. A second level would need a second colour, and the palette has one alert colour on purpose.

When to use it

The threshold alert on the dashboard: the retention driver of the whole product, the moment Brio tells the operator something before they had to ask. Three screens.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. The action inside the banner is outline, not solid. The banner already carries the urgency in its tag and its glyph, and a solid button here would compete with the action of the card beside it. One solid action per zone, and the banner is its own zone.

Not this, use Status pill instead. Do not use an alert to report the ordinary state of things. A cycle that is simply open is a Status pill.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive itself. The button inside it is a Button and carries the states.
