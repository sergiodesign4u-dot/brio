Atom

Media slot

A framed box that holds a photograph or a product screenshot, and holds its space before the image arrives.

Anatomy

  • .wf-mediathe frame: a recessed fill, a 16px radius, a minimum height so the layout does not jump
  • .wf-media imgthe image itself, cropped to fill rather than letterboxed
  • .wf-media.shotthe taller step, for a product screenshot which needs to be legible

Variants and sizes

Size

Two. The resting size holds a photograph; the taller one holds a screenshot of the product, which has text in it and cannot be small.

default
a photograph

shot
a product screenshot

The empty cells, and why they are empty

A third size, tall, was declared in the kit at stage 07 and worn by no screen in either the coloured sample or the grey product. It was deleted at step 2 rather than kept as a variant waiting for a use.

When to use it

The home page and the sign up panel. Photography carries attribute A5 where the product is explained, and it deliberately does not go inside the operator or employee interior: a photograph of strangers on the screen where a person reports a hard week is decoration, and decoration is what attribute A3 forbids.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. One image per slot, always with alt text that says what is in it. The generation prompt and the colour grade for every own image are in design/visuals/README.md, so the style is reproducible.

Not this, use Icon slot instead. Do not put an icon in a media slot. A glyph on a plate is an Icon slot and has a fill of its own.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive.
