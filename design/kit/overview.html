Stage 08

Tokens and components

The same look, a new architecture. The flat kit of stage 07 is split into two levels of token and one file per component, and the whole of it is proved on the same sample of screens. Nothing was redrawn: every visible difference between yesterday and today is named in one of three lists, and the proof is on the verification page.
