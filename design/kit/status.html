Atom

Status pill

A small pill that says what state something is in. It looks like a Chip and never responds to a press.

Anatomy

OpenClosedNot answered
  • .wf-statusthe pill: micro type, secondary ink, a 3:1 boundary
  • .wf-status.onthe live one, which takes primary ink and an action coloured border

Variants and sizes

Emphasis

Two values, and the second one means "this is the one happening now": an open cycle, the current program.

Closed

default
a state that is over or not started

Open

active
the one happening now

The empty cells, and why they are empty

No size axis and no content axis. A status pill is a word, and the word is fixed by the voice dictionary.

When to use it

On the dashboard beside a cycle, in the roster beside a person, on a program card. It answers "what is the state of this" without the operator opening anything, which is half of the two minute review the owner is promised.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Use it for a state the system knows. The words come from the dictionary in voice.md: open, closed, answered, not answered.

Not this, use Chip instead. Do not make it pressable. The moment a person can click it, it is a Chip and has to look and behave like one.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive: no hover, no focus, no disabled. A hover on a status pill would be an invented state, and focus-visible would not fire on it at all.
