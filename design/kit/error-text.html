The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis and no icon. The alert colour and the position are the signal; adding a mark beside it would repeat the same thing twice.

When to use it

Under a field that failed validation, and at the top of a form that failed to save. Seven screens. It always names the next step, because principle 4 says an honest signal still leaves the person with something to do.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Say what happened and what to try, in that order, and keep it to one sentence. Never "successfully" and never an exclamation, both are forbidden by the voice rules.

Not this, use State block instead. If the whole screen failed rather than one field, use a State block with the error variant: it carries a title, a sentence and an action together.
