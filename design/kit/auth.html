When to use it

Sign up and log in. It is the first surface a new operator meets, and the panel beside the form is where the privacy promise is made before a single field is filled in: earn trust before asking for data, which is principle 3.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. The panel goes away first when the screen narrows. On a phone, signing up is one column and nothing competes with it.

Not this, use Privacy strip instead. Do not put the promise inside the form as a hint. One sentence in the flow of a screen is a Privacy strip; this panel is the argument at the moment of the decision.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive itself. The proof row strips the plate off the Icon slot and leaves the glyph; a pair of width and height that also sat in that override was dead in the flat kit and was removed at step 5 rather than allowed to come alive under the new load order.
