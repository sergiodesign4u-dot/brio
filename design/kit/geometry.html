Foundations

Geometry

Spacing, radius, line width and the sizes of things. None of it gets a semantic level, and not because it matters less: a radius has nothing to override. A theme replaces colour, so colour goes through a role; geometry simply repeats, so a component reads the primitive directly.

Measured on the shape of plate A, in its UI component zone. --radius-md is every control, --radius-lg every card and every media frame, --radius-full the pill shapes, --radius-xs one bar of the trend spark, the smallest shape in the language.

Line width

--line-1 1px, every border in the product
--line-2 2px, the focus ring and the current tab underline

There is no shadow token, because there is no shadow in the product. The only box-shadow anywhere is the inset ring on a checked radio option, and it is a line drawn inside a box rather than a shadow. That is worth stating rather than leaving to be discovered: in the dark theme a shadow separates nothing, and a system that leaned on one would have to find a second mechanism at exactly the wrong moment.

Layout helpers

Seven selectors in design/system/layout.css. They arrange things and carry no identity: no colour, no type, no radius. They are listed because a selector in the system that appears in no list is a decision nobody made, and they get no component page because there is nothing to document beyond what they arrange.

Breakpoints, and why they are still literals

Fourteen media queries in the system read 520, 640, 700, 720, 760 and 900 pixels as plain numbers. That is deliberate and it is the one class of literal this stage does not close: responsive is stage 10, and naming a breakpoint here would mean deciding it here. They are counted so that stage 10 finds them in one list instead of hunting.
