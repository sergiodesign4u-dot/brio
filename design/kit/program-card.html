The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis: no programme is highlighted over another. A recommended programme is said in words, not by making its card louder.

When to use it

The library, where the operator picks what to run. Nine screens. It is where the main job is done: a person with no HR training is told what to run and when, and the card carries exactly the four things needed to choose.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Length and effort always visible. That is what an operator with no methodology compares on, and hiding it behind a click is what makes the choice feel like a guess.

Not this, use Pulse card instead. A programme that is already running is not a card in a grid. It is the state on the dashboard, in a Pulse card and a Status pill.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive: the action inside it is a Button. The grid it sits in reflows at 240px per column, and that width is a token, --size-progcard. It was referenced and never declared in the stage 07 kit, so the whole grid declaration was invalid and the cards stacked full width. Declaring it is one of the three named changes of this stage.
