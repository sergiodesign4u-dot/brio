When to use it

The security and privacy page and the sign up flow. It is the long form of what the Privacy strip says in one line, and it is where the trust argument is made in full.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Each row is a mechanism, not a value. "A minimum of five" is a mechanism; "we take privacy seriously" is a value and is forbidden by the voice rules.

Not this, use Privacy strip instead. One sentence in the flow of a screen is a Privacy strip. This component is for the place where the whole model is laid out.
