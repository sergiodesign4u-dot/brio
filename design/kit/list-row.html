Content

Two: name and state, or name and state and a way in. The link appears where there is a screen behind the row.

The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis. A row never turns red for a person who has not answered: that would be exactly the individual judgement the privacy model exists to prevent.

When to use it

The roster, on 19 screens. It says who is on the team and whether the cycle reached them. It does not say what anybody answered, and there is no drill-down into a row, permanently, because that breaks the aggregate-only promise.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. The state on a row is about delivery, not about content: answered or not answered, never how. That distinction is the whole product.

Not this, use Card instead. A row that opens something is not a card. If a row needs a heading and a body, the thing you want is a Card.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive as a row. The row link inside it is a text link and takes its states from base.css.
