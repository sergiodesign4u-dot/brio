The empty cells, and why they are empty

No size axis beyond the compact button inside the public shell, and no emphasis axis: there is one bar.

When to use it

97 of 99 screens. The two without it are the system screens, where the product is not available and navigation would lead nowhere.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. The employee shell has no navigation on purpose. A person answering a check-in is not being invited to explore the product, and giving them a way into the operator side would be a promise the permissions do not keep.

Not this, use Bottom tab bar instead. Below 720px the tabs are not squeezed, they are replaced. The sections move to a Bottom tab bar, which is a separate component with its own page.

States

The tabs and the settings link are text links: they darken their ink on hover and take the standard focus ring. The current tab is a state, not a variant, and it is the only place in the product with an underline in the action colour.

No snapshots here, and that is the rule rather than an omission. The unit of a snapshot is a difference, not an occurrence. This component answers every state exactly as Button does, so the answer is written in this line instead of proved again with pictures. A matrix with more snapshots than named differences is a list of occurrences, not documentation.
