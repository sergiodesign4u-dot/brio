Atom

Icon slot

A soft accent plate with a Solar glyph centred in it. The only place in the product where an icon gets a background.

Anatomy

  • .wf-icothe plate: 56px, a 16px radius, the soft accent fill
  • .wf-ico.roundthe same plate as a circle
  • .wf-ico.smthe small step, 40px with an 8px radius
  • .wf-ico::beforethe glyph, 24px, painted through a mask in strong ink

Variants and sizes

Size

Two, and the container decides: a card in a three up row takes the default, a proof line beside text takes the small one.

default
a card on a marketing page

small
a proof line

Shape

Two, and it is the only shape axis in the system. The round form is used where the row is about people rather than about features.

square
a feature card

round
a four up row about the product

When to use it

The marketing pages, where three or four cards each need a mark that says what they are about, and the sign up panel, where three proof lines carry the privacy promise.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Take the glyph from the icons page rather than from another screen. The plate colour is fixed and the glyph reads at 9.02 on it.

Not this, use Media slot instead. Do not use an icon slot to show a photograph or a screenshot. That is a Media slot, which has a different radius and no fill of its own.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive. Under 520px it takes the same smaller step as the Outcome badge, and that rule now lives here rather than in a shared variable, because the shared variable was resizing this component by accident.
