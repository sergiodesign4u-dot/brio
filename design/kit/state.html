When to use it

Seventeen screens, and six named states: empty, error, waiting, no programme, link expired, already answered. It is the component that carries principle 2 more than any other: every one of these screens says what happened, why, and what to do next.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Always all three parts. A title without a next step leaves the operator exactly where they were, which is the feeling the product exists to remove.

Not this, use Outcome badge instead. A screen that has one thing to report and nothing to do next is an Outcome badge with a heading, not a state block.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive: the button inside it is a Button. The error mark is drawn from the aria-label of the surrounding region rather than from a class, because the coloured copy may not add a class to markup that belongs to the grey original.
