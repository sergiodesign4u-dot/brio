The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis. It arranges and carries no identity, which is also why it is the only organism with a single rule in its file.

When to use it

33 screens: the dashboard, the roster, the library. It is the frame the operator reads inside.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. The rail carries context, never the action the screen exists for. On a phone the rail goes below the main column, and anything essential in it would fall under the fold.

Not this, use Card instead. A row of equal cards on a marketing page is not this component. Those are layout helpers, wf-grid3 and wf-grid4, and they are listed on the geometry page.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive. It loads last in index.css because it holds Lists, State blocks and Pulse cards, and a container loaded above its contents wins the cascade over them.
