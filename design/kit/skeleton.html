Atom

Skeleton

A grey bar standing in for content that has not arrived. It is a state of a screen made visible, not a decoration.

Anatomy

  • .wf-skelone line, 14px tall, the skeleton fill
  • .wf-skel.talla block, 52px, standing in for a card
  • .wf-skel.actthe shape of a button, 190 by 40
  • .wf-skel.w70 / .w50 / .w44the width of a line, which is what makes it look like text

Variants and sizes

Size

Three, and each one is the shape of the thing that is coming: a line of text, a block, an action.

line
a paragraph

block
a card

action
a button

The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis and no animation. A pulsing skeleton is stage 11 and would be motion added before the system had a motion token.

When to use it

The two loading screens: the dashboard while the score is being read, and the check-in while the answer is being sent. It holds the space the content will take, so nothing jumps when it arrives.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Match the shape of what is loading. Three lines of different widths read as a paragraph; one block reads as a card.

Not this, use State block instead. Do not use a skeleton where nothing is coming. An empty list is a State block with a sentence and a next step.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive: it is itself a state. Its fill is measured at 1.2 against the surface and accepted, because it stands in for content rather than conveying any.
