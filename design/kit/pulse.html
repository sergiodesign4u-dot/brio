When to use it

The dashboard, and inside the proof disclosure on the screens that explain what the owner can see. Nine screens. Principle 1 lives here: a number never appears without its interpretation, and the interpretation is in plain language rather than in a scale nobody was taught.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. Number, meaning, next step, in that order, always all three. A dip is called a dip: "Down from 74. Worth a look." A rise states the fact and does not celebrate.

Not this, use Metric instead. A number that needs no interpretation, like the size of a team, is a Metric.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive. The embedded context is declared here rather than added as a class in the markup, because the markup of a coloured copy belongs to the frozen grey original and a copy may not add a class to it.
