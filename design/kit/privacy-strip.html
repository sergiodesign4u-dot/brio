The empty cells, and why they are empty

No emphasis axis, and that is the point. The strip never shouts and never changes colour: it is a mechanism being stated, not a warning.

When to use it

This is the product. The privacy boundary is the differentiator, and it is made visible in the interface rather than promised in a policy. It stands before every check-in, on every operator screen that shows a score, and on the public pages that explain the model.

The rule, and the anti rule

Do this. One specific sentence, from the voice rules: aggregate only, a minimum of five, no live count. Never a vague reassurance and never a link to a policy in place of the sentence.

Not this, use Alert banner instead. Do not use it to warn about something going wrong. That is an Alert banner, which carries a tag and an action.

States

No states: this component is not interactive. Not interactive, except for the optional link inside it, which is a text link and takes its states from base.css.
