Typography

Two families and one scale. Inter Tight carries the voice, Inter carries the reading. Ten static steps and six responsive ones, and every one of them is a step of a scale rather than a name for a place, because the place is what the class is for.

The two families

Inter Tight, the display family

--font-display headings, the wordmark, the score, a card title. Decision of the user at stage 06, chosen against the character of plate A.

Inter, the reading family. Everything a person actually reads, at 15 and 16 pixels, with a line height of 1.55.

--font-body body, labels, controls, micro copy.

The scale, with real product text on every step

Each line is set in the token beside it, and each sample is a real line from voice/docs/microcopy.md, not filler. Sixteen purposeful names at stage 07 became ten scale steps here and nothing moved by a pixel: the purpose lives in the class that reads the step.

Numbers do not shift as they change

font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1 is set on the body in base.css, so every digit occupies the same width. A score that moves from 74 to 68 must not make the card twitch. That is attribute A4, and it is one line of css.
