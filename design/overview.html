Stage 07, UI and Visual

The product in colour

The sample assembled at stage 07: seven screens with their states, built from the component kit rather than drawn. The rest of the product is still grey and waits for the roll-out at stage 12, which is honest work rather than a gap: a screen is built once, when everything it needs already exists.

24 of 99 state pages are in colour, across 13 of 47 screens. The bar above is that number and not a rounding of it.

Why these seven

The sample carries the whole tail

A screen that adds no new component was not taken: it proves nothing. What the sample had to cover was all three levels of the component ladder, the heaviest layouts, a form, a list, and an empty or error state.

Dashboard 4.0 and its six states. The etalon: the operator shell, the pulse card, the trend spark, the privacy strip, and the richest state set in the product, including loading and error. The employee check-in, all eight screens. The other shell, the other person, the disclosure that turns the promise into proof, and the outcome badge. Home 0.0. The public shell, the hero, the four column footer, and the two media slots, which is where attribute A5 finally reaches a product screen. Programs 5.0 and its empty state. Chips, the program card, a grid that reflows, and an empty state that is not an error. Team 6.0 and the manage dialog. The list and its rows, status pills, the copy field, and the dialog, which is one component standing in for six. Sign up 2.0 and its error. The auth shell, a form with several fields, and the error state of a form rather than of a page. Contact 1.3 and its confirmation. The last form primitive, the textarea, and a confirmation that states the fact without celebrating.

Saturation. The kit stopped growing on the sixth screen: Sign up and Contact added no new component, only new arrangements of the ones already there. That is the signal the pack asks for, and it is why the sample stops at seven rather than running on.
