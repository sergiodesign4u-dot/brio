Block bank

Block bank by page type

Where the composition of a page comes from. Thirty nodes are eight to ten page types, so the pass is one per type, not one per node. Two sources answer two different questions and neither is enough alone: competitors give the domain truth (what a company in our category actually puts on this page), Refero gives the craft truth (how a page of this type is built in best-in-class products outside it). A block that traces to no job, barrier or SEO requirement is not taken. Full bank with tracing per block: ia/docs/blocks.md.

The honest gap, named rather than papered over. Refero holds no screen of the type "what your employer can and cannot see" as a product surface, and no pre-question privacy disclosure: the closest craft reference is a marketing security page. The competitor half is worse, because the one competitor with a genuinely specific anonymity model explains it in a help-centre article, outside the product. For types T3 and T9 the craft half is therefore thin, and the composition is carried by our own research instead. This is the one place in the bank where "where we are better" is not a comparison but a hole in the category.
